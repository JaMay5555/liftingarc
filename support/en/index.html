For questions, technical issues, or feedback about LiftingArc, you can contact us by email.

Helpful details

To help us understand a technical issue faster, you may voluntarily include the following information:

  • your iPhone or iPad model
  • your iOS or iPadOS version
  • your LiftingArc version
  • a short description of the steps that led to the issue

Please do not send health data, training data, photos, or videos unless they are necessary for your request.
