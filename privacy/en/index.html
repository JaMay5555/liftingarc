Privacy Policy for LiftingArc

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Controller

Jannis Mayland
Email: liftingarc@outlook.com

Basic principle

LiftingArc works without a user account. The app does not contain advertising, analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or tracking. We do not operate an app server. We do not receive your training, bodyweight, health, or video data.

The only exceptions are an OpenPowerlifting search that you intentionally start, an export that you create yourself, and voluntary email support contact. These cases are explained below.

Locally stored app data

LiftingArc stores data you enter, select, or import in the protected app area on your device. This may include:

  • workouts, exercises, sets, weights, repetitions, duration, and RPE
  • training notes, templates, and training blocks
  • bodyweight entries
  • saved competition data from OpenPowerlifting
  • selected videos and related timing or analysis data
  • app settings

This data is not transferred to us or to a server operated by us.

Apple Health

Using Apple Health is optional and requires your explicit permission through Apple's system dialog.

With your permission, LiftingArc reads the following data:

  • workouts
  • sleep times and sleep stages
  • heart rate and resting heart rate
  • heart rate variability (HRV)
  • active energy and steps

For cardio workouts, LiftingArc may also process the time, duration, activity type, distance, and energy burned. Sleep and recovery values are evaluated and displayed on the device. For a cardio workout, imported measurements, including a condensed heart-rate history, may be stored locally with the workout.

LiftingArc does not write data to Apple Health. Apple Health data is not sent to us, OpenPowerlifting, or other external services and is not used for advertising or marketing. Cardio measurements imported from Apple Health are not included in the manual LiftingArc export.

You can revoke Health access at any time in iOS settings or in the Health app. After that, LiftingArc can no longer access the revoked data types. The remaining app features continue to work.

Videos and photo library

Videos are selected only when you open Apple's system picker yourself and choose a video. LiftingArc does not receive general access to your photo library.

The selected video is copied into the local app area. Playback, repetition timing, and bar path analysis are performed on the device. Videos and analysis results are not sent to us or to an external analysis service.

You can delete videos in the app. Related local analysis and timing data is removed as well. Videos belonging to a deleted workout initially remain with that workout in the local trash and are removed when the workout is permanently deleted.

OpenPowerlifting

The OpenPowerlifting feature is used only when you start an athlete search yourself.

In that case, LiftingArc sends an HTTPS request to openpowerlifting.org. The request contains an identifier derived from the athlete name you entered. The server may process technically necessary connection data such as IP address, time, requested address, and app identifier.

Your own workouts, bodyweight data, Apple Health data, videos, and other training data are not sent to OpenPowerlifting. Public competition results are shown first as a preview and are stored locally in LiftingArc only if you choose to save them. Saved athletes can be deleted in the app.

OpenPowerlifting is an independent external provider. Its own terms apply to its processing and retention of technical connection data.

Import, export, and cloud backups

CSV or backup files selected by you are processed locally.

A full backup export is created only when you start it yourself. It may include workouts, exercises, templates, training blocks, bodyweight entries, saved competition data, removed sets, and set videos. You choose where the export file is saved through Apple's file dialog. If you select iCloud Drive or another storage provider, the file is stored by that provider according to your choice. LiftingArc does not receive access to the exported file.

Note for TestFlight: Older TestFlight versions may additionally contain an optional iCloud backup feature. It works only if you choose a folder yourself and enable automatic backup. This automatic cloud backup is not intended for the public App Store build.

You are responsible for deleting exported files and existing TestFlight backups from the location where you saved them if you no longer need them.

Email support

If you voluntarily contact us by email, we process your email address, technical email headers, and the content and possible attachments of your message to answer your request.

The support mailbox is provided through Microsoft Outlook. Microsoft may process data according to its own privacy statement.

Support messages are stored only as long as needed to process the request and any necessary follow-up questions, unless legal obligations or required proof make longer retention necessary. You can request deletion of your support message at any time via liftingarc@outlook.com.

Please do not send health data, training data, photos, or videos by email unless they are necessary for your request.

Use of this website

These privacy and support pages are provided through GitHub Pages. When you visit them, GitHub may process technically necessary data such as IP address, time, requested page, and browser information to deliver and protect the site. More information is available in GitHub's privacy statement.

These pages do not use our own analytics tools, advertising services, external fonts, or marketing cookies.

Storage and deletion

Local app data remains on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall LiftingArc. Deleted workouts initially remain in the local workout trash, where they can be restored or permanently deleted.

Deleting the app removes its local app area from the device. Manually exported files, older TestFlight backups, and device backups managed by the operating system may need to be deleted separately.

Because we do not operate an app server or user accounts, we cannot view or remotely delete app data stored only on your device.

Your privacy rights

Where applicable privacy law provides these rights, you may request access, rectification, deletion, restriction of processing, data portability, or object to processing. You may withdraw consent with effect for the future.

For data stored only locally on your device, you can exercise these options directly by editing or deleting data in the app or by uninstalling the app. For support or website requests, contact us at liftingarc@outlook.com. You may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

Changes

This Privacy Policy will be updated if LiftingArc's features or data processing practices change. The current version is available on this page.
