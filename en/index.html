Everything in one place

More than a digital training log.

From a spontaneous session to a structured training block, logging, history, and analysis stay connected.

01

Workouts built for the gym

During training, you capture exactly the information that matters later: weight, reps, duration, RPE, and your own notes. Previous set values remain close by, so there is no need to search through old sessions.

Rest timers, targets, and personal-record cues support the session without interrupting the training flow.

  • Strength, cardio, and mobility workouts
  • Warm-ups, back-off sets, and targets
  • Set videos attached to your training
02

Templates & blocks

Prepare recurring sessions as templates. For longer-term goals, organize several weeks into a training block and plan load, reps, or RPE in advance.

Starting the plan creates an independent workout, so you can adapt it in the gym without changing the original template.

03

History & PRs

Completed sessions remain available as a clear history. For every exercise, you can see how performance, training volume, and personal bests developed over time.

This shows more than individual records: it helps reveal whether weeks of training are moving in the intended direction.

04

Tools for informed decisions

The tools put your training data into context instead of merely collecting numbers. Estimate strength, compare performance, follow bodyweight trends, and work toward concrete goals.

They include 1RM and DOTS calculators, powerlifting and all-time statistics, Goal Engine, bodyweight and cardio insights, plus intentionally started Apple Health and OpenPowerlifting features.

  • Strength & records
  • Goals & bodyweight
  • Cardio & recovery
  • Competition & comparison
05

Video analysis beyond guesswork

Set videos can be attached directly to a workout or imported independently from the gallery. This keeps the recording, exercise, and training context connected.

Time repetitions manually or examine bar movement using anchors you place yourself. Automatically detected results can be reviewed and corrected afterwards.

  • Videos collected in one gallery
  • Manual repetition timing
  • Transparent, correctable analysis
06

Your data stays yours

Workouts, exercises, templates, videos, and confirmed analyses are stored locally on your device. There is currently no required account, premium gate, or subscription.

You start exports and restores deliberately. External services are contacted only for features you intentionally open.
