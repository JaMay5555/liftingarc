Local-first

Training is personal. So is data storage.

Workouts, exercises, templates, videos, and confirmed analyses are stored locally on your device. LiftingArc does not create automatic cloud backups or show advertising.

  • Manual export and restore
  • No premium gate and no subscription
  • External requests only for features you intentionally start
